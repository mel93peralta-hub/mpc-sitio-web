FAQ

What people ask us most.

How much do I need to spend on ads?

It depends on your industry, your area, and your goal. We set it with you during the audit. You pay the ad spend directly to Meta or Google, separate from our fee, and you always know what's being spent.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

We work under a clear contract with a minimum term stated in writing before we start. No fine print: from day one you know what's included and how long it runs.

Is this a fit if I'm new to digital marketing?

Yes. What matters isn't the size of your business or the price of what you sell, but the stage you're at. If you don't have a system to answer messages yet, we start with the basics.

How soon will it be running?

The full system is ready in about 10 business days: ads, messaging, calendar, and reminders. You approve everything before we go live.

Do you guarantee results?

We don't promise guaranteed results. Every month we show you what we measure: messages, appointments, show rate, and cost per appointment, and we adjust based on that data.

Who owns the accounts and the data?

You do. Ad accounts, pages, contacts, and data stay in your business's name.

Do you work in Spanish and English?

Yes. We answer and build ads in both languages, never mixed, so each audience gets a message made for them.
